Before You Decide

Is this official exam prep?

No. It is an independent illustrated study guide. It covers the topics the national certification exams ask about — NHA CPT, ASCP PBT, AMT RPT and NCCT — but Studio Facile is not affiliated with any of those organizations, and the 150 practice questions were written for this book: they are not official exam questions. Each exam sets its own length, timing and passing standard, so check your candidate handbook.

Does it replace my phlebotomy program?

No. It is not a course, and it does not give you a certificate or clinical hours. It is the illustrated material that goes with the training you are already doing.

Which standards does it follow?

The CLSI standards and common US practice. The tube colors are BD Vacutainer’s, the most common tube line in US labs. Where laboratories differ, the page says so — and your facility’s procedure manual always has the final word.
