Questions

Before You Decide

Is this official VTNE material?

No. It is an independent illustrated study guide built on the VTNE content outline. The VTNE is a program of the AAVSB, and Studio Facile is not affiliated with or endorsed by the AAVSB. The 150 practice questions were written for this book; they are not official exam questions.

Does it replace my vet tech program?

No. It is not a course, it does not give credit or clinical hours, and it does not replace your program’s textbooks. It is the illustrated material that goes with them.

Does it tell me what dose to give?

No. The book covers drug classes and how they are used, and the workbook teaches the calculation with example numbers. The dose is always set by the veterinarian and the product label, and your clinic’s protocols come first.

How do I get it?

By email, straight after you buy. Three PDFs you can read on your phone, tablet or computer.

Can I print it?

Yes, as many times as you want, for yourself. The practice test has an answer sheet to print and fill in.
