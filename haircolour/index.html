It Is Not a Lack of Talent. Nobody Ever Showed You Why Colour Turns Out the Way It Does.

You learnt it by heart: violet for yellow, blue for orange, green for red. Then the client sits down in your chair...

...and in that moment you cannot remember which one cancels which. They were just colours on a list.

Here, neutralising fits on one page: each pair from the colour wheel beside the hair it puts right. A violet tone takes the yellow out of a pale blonde, a blue-based ash calms orange, a green-based matt tone dulls unwanted red.

And behind it sits one rule: neutralising does not lighten. It only turns an unwanted tone into a neutral one at the same depth.

First you understand the rule. The right formula follows.
